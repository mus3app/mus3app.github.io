Questions

Do I have to import my library?

No. Point Mus3 at your folders and start listening. Nothing is copied or moved. Ratings, tags and artwork saved in your files show up as they are.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. Mus3 plays files on your Mac. No account, no sign‑in. It goes online only for what you ask for: cover art, lyrics, tag lookup, radio.

What does Mus3 do with my files?

Nothing until you say so. Tags, ratings and artwork are written when you edit them. Files move only when you send or organise them.

What happens after the first year?

Mus3 keeps working, for good. You keep every version released during your year. Newer ones are a discounted renewal, only if you want them.

Will it be on the Mac App Store?

Later, and with fewer features because of App Store rules. The full version lives here.

What about cracked copies?

A cracked Mus3 is someone else’s program with our name on it. We can’t tell you what it does on your Mac.
