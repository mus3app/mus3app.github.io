Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Mus3 has no account, no sign‑in and no analytics. Your library, play counts and settings stay on your Mac.

The app

Mus3 goes online only for features you use:

  • Tag lookup and cover art: MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, iTunes Search.
  • Lyrics: LRCLIB.
  • Radio: Radio Browser, SomaFM, and the stations you play.
  • Artist pictures: Wikimedia Commons.
  • Updates: a check for a new version on mus3.app.

These requests carry what is needed to answer them, such as an artist and album name. They never include your library, your files or anything that identifies you.

This site

mus3.app uses no cookies and no trackers.

Buying

Payments are handled by our reseller, who acts as the seller of record. We receive your name, email and country to issue your licence key. We never see your card.
